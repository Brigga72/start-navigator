Built for your cruise

Cruise Navigator keeps your ship navigation, schedule, CocoCay plans, favorite destinations, and onboard tools together in one iPhone-friendly app.

Navigation

Routes use the verified walking network across the mapped ship decks with Balanced, Elevators Preferred, and No Stairs routing choices. Follow the highlighted route and use onboard signs and crew instructions whenever conditions differ.

Offline ready

Core app screens, navigation data, and deck maps are stored for offline use after the app has been loaded on your device.
