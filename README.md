# Cruise Navigator v0.10.0

Personal iPhone-first cruise companion for Royal Caribbean Star of the Seas, personalized around Cabin 7456.

## v0.10
- Added My Schedule for shows, dining, excursions, activities, and other reservations.
- Show picker auto-links known Star of the Seas show venues.
- Day/time, arrive-early preference, notes, edit/delete, and attended status persist locally.
- Next-up card on Home and Schedule.
- Leave-by reminder and Take Me There route button for mapped venues.
- Retains Star navigation, Explore, CocoCay, Hideaway access, and update handling.

All schedule entries are stored locally on the device. Sailing-specific showtimes and reservation availability should be checked in the Royal Caribbean app.
